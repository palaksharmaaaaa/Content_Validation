## 1. Inquiry Context & Architectural Mandate

### 1.1 Architectural Mandate
Digital media verification requires moving beyond brittle heuristic checks and isolated neural discriminators. The objective of this specification is to establish an end-to-end, multi-layered analytical framework for the `project-content-validation` engine. The system must ingest any arbitrary digital image asset, identify its position across all operative signal and semantic dimensions, evaluate physical and mathematical consistency, inspect cryptographic provenance, detect adversarial evasion and security exploits, and output a calibrated Bayesian posterior probability of authenticity alongside clear provenance assertions.

### 1.2 Open-Class Nature & Known Boundaries
1. **Unbounded Generative Horizon:** Generative modeling is an open-ended adversarial problem. Foundation model weights, diffusion schedulers, and post-processing tools are continuously updated. No detector can guarantee 100% certainty on arbitrary unseen inputs.
2. **The Calibrated Probability Principle:** Under Bayesian evidence pooling ($\mathcal{L} = \ln \frac{P(\text{AI})}{P(\text{Real})}$), the logistic sigmoid $P(\text{AI} \mid \mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\mathcal{L}}}$ asymptotically approaches 0 or 1 but mathematically never reaches absolute $0.00\%$ or $100.00\%$. The engine explicitly defines calibrated confidence intervals ($P \ge 0.995$ as Highly Confident AI, $P \le 0.005$ as Highly Confident Real) and reserves the range $0.40 \le P \le 0.60$ as `AMBIGUOUS / UNCERTAIN`.
3. **Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Detection:** When an asset's feature embeddings deviate from both known authentic camera distributions and known generative foundation model clusters by more than a Mahalanobis distance threshold $D_M > \tau_{\text{OOD}}$, the engine flags the asset as `UNVERIFIED / OUT-OF-DISTRIBUTION (OOD) / UNSEEN GENERATOR`, rather than forcing a misleading binary decision.
4. **Context vs. Content:** A photograph may be physically authentic (genuine camera capture with verified PRNU and unbroken metadata) yet simultaneously be weaponized as severe misinformation via out-of-context captioning or recycled historical placement (a "cheapfake"). Provenance validation must decouple physical signal authenticity from contextual and semantic truth claims.

### 18.1 Lossy Transform Codecs & Forensic Signatures
*   **JPEG / JFIF:** $8\times 8$ Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT). Quantization matrices in DQT markers encode compression aggressiveness; specific software encoders (Photoshop, libjpeg, camera firmware) have unique DQT table fingerprints.
*   **Double JPEG Compression & Ghosting:** When an image is modified and re-saved, DCT coefficients are doubly quantized. Mismatched block grids between the original and spliced region produce detectable periodicity and "ghost" artifacts in difference images.
*   **Error Level Analysis (ELA):** Measures the rate of error degradation upon re-saving at a fixed quality factor ($90\%$). Authentic regions display uniform error variance; spliced or AI-inpainted patches display anomalous error levels.
*   **HEIC / HEIF (High Efficiency Image File Format):** ISO Base Media File Format container (`ftypheic`, `mif1`). Supports tiled image grids, alpha transparency auxiliary items, and burst sequences.
*   **AVIF (AV1 Image File Format):** Open intra-frame AV1 coding supporting 10-bit/12-bit color, HDR PQ/HLG curves, and superior high-frequency detail retention.
*   **JPEG XL (JXL):** Next-gen format supporting both VarDCT mode (ultra-efficient lossy) and Modular mode (lossless, responsive), capable of losslessly transcoding existing legacy JPEGs with $20\%$ size reduction.

### 18.2 RAW Sensor Formats & Camera Calibration
*   **Camera RAW (DNG, Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fujifilm RAF):** Unprocessed sensor photodiode readings prior to demosaicing; contains sensor CFA layout, BlackLevel, WhiteLevel, ColorMatrix calibration profiles, and vendor-proprietary MakerNotes. Tampering with MakerNotes or injecting synthetic demosaicing gradients breaks raw integrity.

### 18.3 Auxiliary Layers, Gain Maps, & Depth Maps
*   **HDR Gain Maps (ISO 21496-1 / Ultra HDR):** A base SDR image paired with an auxiliary single-channel gain map image that dynamically scales luminance on HDR-capable displays. Discrepancies between base SDR and gain map indicate selective tampering.
*   **Monocular Depth Maps:** Embedded auxiliary channels in smartphone captures (Apple Portrait Mode, Google Pixel depth) calculating per-pixel depth ($Z$). Tampered foreground objects disrupt depth boundary alignment.

## 21. Dedicated Metadata, Container-Structure, & Parser Forensics

Image metadata and container bitstreams provide forensic vectors that corroborate or contradict pixel-level physical signals.

> **Evidentiary Weight:** Metadata is trivially forgeable and is routinely stripped by benign platforms (messaging apps, social upload pipelines, screenshots, privacy tools). Absence of EXIF/MakerNotes/ICC data therefore does NOT indicate synthesis, and presence does NOT indicate authenticity. These signals enter the Bayesian LLR fusion as low-weight evidence and never as a sole determinant.

### 21.1 EXIF, XMP, & IPTC Structural Analysis
*   **Tag Consistency & Schema Validation:** Comparing Exchangeable Image File Format (EXIF 2.32), Extensible Metadata Platform (XMP), and IPTC Photo Metadata records. Inconsistencies between EXIF `Model` and XMP `LensModel`, or mismatched schema namespaces, expose careless metadata grafting.
*   **Timestamp Sanity & Temporal Plausibility:** Cross-verifying `DateTimeOriginal`, `DateTimeDigitized`, `ModifyDate`, and `GPSDateStamp`. Temporal paradoxes (e.g., `DateTimeDigitized` preceding `DateTimeOriginal`, or GPS timestamps lagging UTC camera clocks by hours) indicate metadata manipulation or file splicing.
*   **MakerNotes & Proprietary Camera Calibration:** Camera manufacturers (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Apple) embed proprietary binary MakerNotes containing shutter count, internal sensor temperature, focus distance brackets, and cryptographic lens calibration curves. Third-party photo editors and generative synthesis scripts strip or corrupt MakerNotes.

### 21.2 Thumbnail & Embedded Preview Discrepancies
*   **EXIF / IFD1 Thumbnail Desynchronization:** Most camera firmwares embed a low-resolution ($160\times 120$) preview JPEG in IFD1 or Adobe XMP. Image editors frequently modify or crop the main full-resolution image while failing to update the embedded thumbnail, preserving the original unedited scene inside the file's own preview buffer.
*   **Embedded JUMBF / C2PA Preview Boxes:** Inspecting embedded C2PA thumbnail assets against the primary image raster to detect tampered presentation assets.

### 21.3 Color Profiles & ICC Tag Integrity
*   **ICC Profile Fingerprints:** Authenticating International Color Consortium (ICC) profiles embedded in image containers (JPEG `APP2` segments, PNG `iCCP` chunks, HEIF/AVIF `colr` boxes). Genuine camera profiles include specific hardware device descriptions, chromatic adaptation matrices, and CIE $D_{50}$ illuminant references; synthetic images typically embed generic sRGB v4 or lack color profile tags entirely.

### 21.4 Software & Encoder Signatures
*   **Quantization & Huffman Code Table Signatures:** Comparing JPEG DQT tables and Huffman trees against known databases of hardware ISPs and software libraries (libjpeg-turbo, Photoshop Save For Web, Pillow, ImageMagick, GIMP).
*   **Software String Fingerprints:** Inspecting EXIF `Software` tags, XMP `CreatorTool` histories, and trailing metadata for remnants of generative tools (e.g., ComfyUI workflow JSON strings, A1111 generation parameters, Midjourney Job IDs) or hex markers indicating command-line post-processing.

### Calibrated Probability Bands
As dictated by the logistic formulation $P = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\mathcal{L}}}$, real-world probabilities asymptote toward but never strictly reach $1.0$ ($100\%$) or $0.0$. Decision boundaries enforce five distinct operational bands:

1.  **High-Confidence Synthetic / AI-Generated ($P(\text{AI}) \ge 0.995$):** Unambiguous generative synthesis artifacts, broken PRNU, or synthetic C2PA assertions.
2.  **Leaning Synthetic / Flagged for Review ($0.600 < P(\text{AI}) < 0.995$):** Notable statistical anomalies or partial generative inpainting detected; automated platform warning applied.
3.  **Inconclusive / Indeterminate Evidence ($0.400 \le P(\text{AI}) \le 0.600$):** Equivocal signals resulting from heavy compression, format conversion, or conflicting multi-feature evidence; flagged for forensic specialist review.
4.  **Leaning Authentic / Low Anomaly ($0.005 < P(\text{AI}) < 0.400$):** Minor non-generative post-processing or standard compression artifacts consistent with physical capture.
5.  **High-Confidence Authentic Physical Capture ($P(\text{AI}) \le 0.005 \iff P(\text{Real}) \ge 0.995$):** Robust physical sensor noise, valid optical wavefront physics, intact MakerNotes, and verified cryptographic hardware provenance.

> **Orthogonal Channels:** The probability bands apply only to the in-distribution authenticity score. Three channels operate independently of it: (a) the Trust & Safety pre-gate (CSAM/NCII hard-blocks) short-circuits all bands; (b) a valid ZK-verified or hardware-signed edit proof is reported as an `AUTHENTIC_EDITED` provenance label alongside, not inside, the band; (c) contextual/cheapfake claims are assessed separately from signal authenticity (Section 22.3).

2.  **Humility in Open-World Forensics:** Recognizing that media creation and synthesis constitute open classes with perpetually evolving generative engines and adversarial attacks, the system explicitly decouples epistemic uncertainty via `OUT-OF-DISTRIBUTION (OOD)` gating and provides an open-set `UNKNOWN_SOURCE` attribution state.
3.  **Multi-Evidence Fusion is Essential:** Physical sensor validation, cryptographic assertions, and statistical learning reinforce one another, preventing single-point failure modes across the content lifecycle.

## Appendix C: Mathematical Physics Formulations

### 1. Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU) & Peak-to-Correlation Energy (PCE)
$$\sigma^2_{\text{PRNU}} = \frac{1}{|M|} \sum_{(x,y) \in M} (W(x,y) - \mu_W)^2 \ge 2.10 \implies \sigma_{\text{PRNU}} \ge 1.45, \quad W = I - F_{\text{Wiener}}(I)$$

$$\text{PCE} = \frac{C(s_{\text{peak}})^2}{\frac{1}{|S| - |\mathcal{N}|} \sum_{s \notin \mathcal{N}} C(s)^2} \ge 50, \quad \rho = \frac{\langle W, K \rangle}{\|W\| \|K\|} \ge 0.15$$
*(Note: $\sigma \ge 1.45$ and $\text{PCE} \ge 50$ represent empirical thresholds on normalized 8-bit uncompressed flat-field reference sets from the Dresden Image Database and VISION dataset; subject to sensor gain and compression scaling).*

### 2. Neuromorphic Event Logarithmic Threshold
$$\Delta \ln(I) = \ln(I(x,y,t)) - \ln(I(x,y,t_{\text{prev}})) \ge \pm C_{\text{th}}$$

### 3. Stokes Polarimetric Vector & Polarization Degrees
$$\mathbf{S} = \begin{bmatrix} S_0 \\ S_1 \\ S_2 \\ S_3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} I_0 + I_{90} \\ I_0 - I_{90} \\ I_{45} - I_{135} \\ I_R - I_L \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{DoLP} = \frac{\sqrt{S_1^2 + S_2^2}}{S_0}, \quad \text{DoP} = \frac{\sqrt{S_1^2 + S_2^2 + S_3^2}}{S_0}$$

### 4. Bayesian Multi-Evidence Pooling LLR & Calibration
$$\mathcal{L} = \sum_{i=1}^N w_i \ln \left( \frac{P(x_i \mid \text{AI})}{P(x_i \mid \text{Real})} \right), \quad P(\text{AI} \mid \mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\mathcal{L}}}$$

**Post-audit corrections (2026-10-03), all covered by tests:**
- Scores are heuristic and uncalibrated (`calibration_status`); thresholds were tuned on synthetic fixtures only. `python -m services.calibration_cli --modality image` measures accuracy, ECE and band occupancy on the held-out validation split once a labeled media library exists.

- The package pipelines and the Streamlit flow share one verdict path (`core.decision.generate_final_decision`, `decision_mode` = `image_authoritative` | `fused`); attribution is explanation only and is not double counted.
- Parser fixes: RIFF chunk walking is seek-based (no false WARN beyond 4 MB), PNG text chunks after IDAT are found.
