# What is checked

Everything the engine examines, per media type. The tables are generated from the check registry (`core/forensics/registry.py`), so they list the checks that actually run. Nothing here is aspirational: a thing that is not in these tables is not checked.

## How a file is judged

1. **Gates** (before anything else, `core/forensics/gates.py`): a SHA-256 block list you supply (inactive until you add one) and recognition of scientific formats the engine cannot score (FITS, DICOM, GeoTIFF, OpenEXR, HDF5, NetCDF, AEDAT). A gate hit stops the analysis and no AI verdict is given.
2. **Profile and provenance**: hashes, geometry, container facts, EXIF/XMP, and a byte-level scan for C2PA markers (presence only; no signature is verified).
3. **Dimension checks, "pre" phase**: the findings below, each isolated so one failure never stops the rest.
4. **Detector**: pixel, spectral or temporal signals combined into a probability (see below), plus an optional trained face classifier for images.
5. **Content and attribution**: what is in the file, and which known generator it resembles (explanation only).
6. **Dimension checks, "post" phase**: reuse fingerprint, rights and privacy flags, re-encoding likelihood, confidence limiters.
7. **Decision** (`core/decision.py`): one verdict, five confidence bands, a nine-dimension dossier and a plain-English explanation.

### What may change the probability

Each finding has an evidence class. Only three classes may add evidence, as small capped terms (base-10 log-odds: at most 0.25 per finding, 0.40 for an explicit generator declaration, 0.40 in total):

| Class | May change the score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `PHYSICAL_SIGNAL` | yes | measured from pixels, samples or frames |
| `LEARNED_SIGNAL` | yes, one direction only | output of a trained classifier (the face check) |
| `METADATA_WEAK` | yes | tags and boxes, which are easy to forge or strip |
| `SECURITY`, `LEGAL_FLAG`, `CONTEXT`, `RELIABILITY` | never | advisory: they report risks and limits |

The absence of a signal (no metadata, no ENF hum, no marker) is never scored.

Three things move the score by more than those capped dimension terms, and are stated here so the cap above is not read as the whole story:

* **The detector's own pooled measurements** (image: sensor-noise residual, surface smoothness, Fourier decay; audio and video: their weighted averages). These are the main signal and are hand-set, not calibrated.
* **A declaration inside the file** (image only). A generator named in EXIF/XMP adds 3.2 and a visible AI watermark adds 3.5 in base-10 log-odds (odds of about 1,600 to 1 and 3,200 to 1) because the file says so itself. The declaration is unauthenticated: anyone can write it, and equally anyone can strip it, so its absence is never scored. When a declared AI file also reads strongly like a real camera capture, the final verdict becomes `UNDETERMINED` instead of picking a side.
* **A dominant face** (image only). If one face covers at least 15 % of the picture and the face classifier scores it 95 % AI-like or higher, the AI score becomes a blend of 35 % pixel detector and 65 % face score, and a score of 65 % or more turns a "real" category into "fully AI generated". A real-looking face never raises confidence.

A finding's status is one of `PASS`, `WARN`, `FAIL`, `INFO`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `NOT_CALIBRATED`, `RECOGNIZED_OOS` or `ERROR`.

## Image

**Detector signals** (`image_detector/features.py`, pooled in `detector.py`): sensor-noise residual (a median-filter residual, not PRNU), surface smoothness, Fourier radial decay, error-level analysis, EXIF and C2PA, a Gemini sparkle watermark, cut-out backgrounds, scanned prints, face-swap filename cues, digital art, screenshots, inpainting, screen re-photography (moiré), spectral modality and canonical AI output sizes. A seven-branch decision tree (`scoring.py`) maps the result to one of nine states (authentic photograph, edited, recaptured screen, screenshot variants, AI-enhanced composite, fully AI-generated, procedural CGI).

**Dimension checks**

| Check | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| `format_sniff` | true format against the extension |
| `trailing_data` | bytes after the end of the image |
| `polyglot_signatures` | archive, executable or script signatures hidden in the file |
| `svg_active_content` | scripts and event handlers in SVG |
| `prompt_injection_text` | instruction-like text in metadata aimed at automated reviewers |
| `jpeg_quant_tables` | quantisation tables, estimated quality, subsampling |
| `png_chunks` | chunk inventory and generator-parameter chunks |
| `exif_consistency` | coherence of camera EXIF (never counted as proof of a camera) |
| `timestamp_sanity` | plausibility of capture and modification times |
| `thumbnail_match` | embedded thumbnail against the pixels |
| `icc_profile` | presence and descriptor of an ICC profile |
| `face_authenticity` | each face judged real or generated by a trained classifier |
| `perceptual_hash` | hashes, matched against a local reference index if you provide one |
| `rights_and_privacy` | copyright notice, GPS, biometric and AI-disclosure labels |
| `platform_reencode` | likelihood the file was re-encoded by a platform |
| `confidence_limiters` | conditions that weaken the signals (small size, heavy compression, screenshots) |

## Audio

**Detector signals** (`audio_detector/features.py`): a high-frequency cutoff typical of neural vocoders, spectral flatness (cut-off and flatness are computed on the FFT magnitude spectrum, not on power, and the audio is decoded to 16 kHz first, so the 15-16.5 kHz vocoder band can only be tested on input analysed at 44.1 kHz or more, which the detector never does), the share of digital silence, and the high-frequency energy ratio, combined as a weighted average. Per-window labels give a timeline.

**Dimension checks**

| Check | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| `format_sniff`, `trailing_data`, `polyglot_signatures`, `prompt_injection_text` | as for images |
| `id3_tags` | ID3v2 structure and software tags |
| `riff_structure` | RIFF/WAV chunk walk, including multiple LIST chunks |
| `flac_md5` | the MD5 stored in a FLAC header against the decoded audio (16- and 24-bit FLAC only; reports when the encoder stored none) |
| `mp3_encoder_tag` | Xing/LAME encoder tag |
| `ogg_structure` | Ogg page integrity |
| `enf_trace` | mains-hum (ENF) trace: a continuous trace slightly lowers the score, a splice raises it, absence is never evidence |
| `fake_hires` | upsampled or lossy-origin audio in a high-rate lossless file (band limit far below Nyquist) and 16-bit audio padded into 24 bits |
| `telephony_channel` | telephone-style band limiting |
| `loudness_dynamics` | loudness and dynamic range |
| `audio_fingerprint` | acoustic fingerprint, matched against a local reference index if you provide one |
| `rights_and_privacy`, `transcoding_cascade`, `confidence_limiters` | as for images |

## Video

**Detector signals** (`video_detector/detector.py`, `temporal.py`): per-frame noise and smoothness, frame-to-frame motion variance (warping, flicker, frozen footage), and brightness flicker, combined as a weighted average. The built-in frame scorer can be replaced by an external frame detector.

**Dimension checks**

| Check | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| `format_sniff`, `polyglot_signatures`, `prompt_injection_text`, `trailing_data` | as above; trailing data means bytes beyond the declared end of the container |
| `isobmff_boxes` | MP4/MOV box layout |
| `isobmff_tables` | sample-table consistency |
| `mp4_metadata` | creation times and tool strings |
| `telemetry_tracks` | hardware telemetry tracks |
| `encoder_sei` | encoder strings inside the bitstream |
| `ebml_info` | Matroska/WebM writer strings |
| `interlacing` | combing |
| `temporal_cadence` | duplicated frames and pulldown |
| `video_fingerprint` | frame hashes, matched against a local reference index if you provide one |
| `rights_and_privacy`, `transcoding_cascade`, `confidence_limiters` | as above |

Interlacing and cadence are informational and never change the score.

## Scene content and minors

`core/perception/` describes what is in an image with pretrained models, and screens for minors (see [Limitations](LIMITATIONS.md#age-and-minor-screening)):

| Job | How |
|---|---|
| Faces | YuNet; a separate recall-first finder with enlarged overlapping tiles for small faces |
| People, animals, vehicles, objects | RF-DETR Small |
| Place, species, vehicle type, genre, time of day | SigLIP 2 Base, zero-shot against fixed vocabularies |
| Expression and same-person matching | OpenCV zoo MobileFaceNet and SFace |
| Apparent age of every person | MiVOLO v2 on face and body, plus a SigLIP 2 age-group opinion; recall-first, so uncertain people go to review |
| Videos | frames chosen adaptively (every content change plus at most one per second, up to 60) |

## Not covered

Recognised but not scored: the scientific formats above. Not implemented: cryptographic C2PA validation, face-morph and presentation-attack detection, OCR, harm classifiers, reverse image search, and analysis of MakerNotes or XMP namespaces.
