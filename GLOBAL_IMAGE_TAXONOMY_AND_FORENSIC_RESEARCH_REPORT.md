## 4. Dimension A: Provenance, Origin, & Authenticity Spectrum

```
                                  [ THE AUTHENTICITY SPECTRUM ]
                                                │
   ┌───────────────────────┬────────────────────┼────────────────────┬───────────────────────┐
   ▼                       ▼                    ▼                    ▼                       ▼
[ State 1: Optical ]    [ State 2: Edit ]    [ State 3: Mix ]     [ State 4: AI ]         [ State 5: Screen ]
Physical Camera Sensor  Conventional Human   Neural Inpainting,   Pure Generative         GPU Framebuffer
PRNU noise, Bayer CFA,  Cutout, Photoshop,   Face Swap, Topaz     Diffusion/Flow, Zero    UI Status Bars,
Natural optics (PSF)    Canva, Typography    Photo AI, Remini     PRNU, Latent artifacts  Zero Sensor Noise
```

### 1. Pure Physical Optical Sensor Capture (`digitalCapture`)
*   **Ontology:** Recorded directly by a single optical exposure on a physical CMOS or CCD sensor through an optical glass lens.
*   **Forensic Physics:**
    *   *PRNU (Photo-Response Non-Uniformity):* Silicon substrate microscopic pixel gain variation producing a deterministic, non-zero noise residual ($\sigma_{\text{flat}} \ge 1.45$).
    *   *Bayer CFA Demosaicing:* Periodic interpolation gradients between Red, Green, and Blue sub-pixels (RGGB, RYYB).
    *   *Optical Point Spread Function (PSF):* Natural diffraction blur, spherical aberration, chromatic fringing at high-contrast edges.
    *   *Metadata:* Unbroken EXIF/TIFF hardware parameters (Camera Make/Model, focal length, aperture f-number, shutter speed, ISO).

### 2. Computational Photography Capture (`computationalCapture`)
*   **Ontology:** In-camera multi-frame burst exposure merged via onboard digital signal processors (DSP) without generative hallucination (Apple Deep Fusion/Photonic Engine, Google HDR+/Night Sight, Samsung Adaptive Pixel).
*   **Forensic Physics:** Local tone-mapping gradients, multi-frame alignment micro-ghosting, preserved physical PRNU across tiles, depth-map calculated bokeh using dual-pixel phase detection.

### 3. Physical Analog Scan / Print Digitization (`print`, `negativeFilm`, `positiveFilm`)
*   **Ontology:** Conversion of a physical chemical or mechanical artifact into a digital raster file via flatbed, drum, or film scanning.
*   **Sub-Types:**
    *   *Color Negative Film (C-41 process):* Orange mask base, silver halide converted to dye clouds, absence of Bayer CFA grids.
    *   *Reversal / Transparency Slide Film (E-6 process, Kodachrome, Velvia):* High optical dynamic range, deep dye density, micro-dust electrostatic fibers.
    *   *Black & White Silver Halide (Tri-X, HP5, T-Max):* Random metallic silver grain clusters (tabular grains), continuous tonal grayscale.
    *   *Historic Chemical Emulsions:* Daguerreotype (silver-plated copper), Tintype/Ferrotype (lacquered iron plate), Cyanotype (Prussian blue iron salts), Platinum/Palladium prints.
    *   *Mechanical Halftone Prints:* Offset lithography, gravure, and letterpress prints showing CMYK rosette screen angles and ink bleeding along paper cellulose fibers.

### 4. Physical Re-Photography / Screen Recapturing
*   **Ontology:** Photographing an active display monitor, television, laptop, or mobile screen with a secondary physical camera.
*   **Forensic Physics:**
    *   *Spatial Moiré Patterns:* High-frequency spatial beating between display subpixel grids and camera sensor pixels.
    *   *Display Subpixel Architecture:* Magnified views reveal RGB stripe, Pentile, or BGR subpixel geometries.
    *   *Geometric Keystone Distortion:* Trapezoidal angular perspective off-axis from display normal.

### 5. Digital Framebuffer Capture / Screenshots (`screenCapture`)
*   **Ontology:** Direct software capture from an operating system display compositor (Windows Desktop Window Manager, macOS Quartz/Metal, Android SurfaceFlinger, Linux Wayland/X11).
*   **Forensic Physics:**
    *   **Zero Sensor Noise:** Total absence of physical optical noise ($\sigma_{\text{flat}} < 0.20$).
    *   *Rectilinear UI Alignment:* Pixel-perfect $90^\circ$ coordinate boundaries, window title bars, dock icons, status bars.
    *   *Subpixel Text Rendering:* Anti-aliased font glyphs rendered via Microsoft ClearType or macOS FreeType.

### 6. Conventional Digital Graphic Design (`minorHumanEdits`, `compositeSynthetic`)
*   **Ontology:** Non-generative manipulation in raster/vector graphic design applications (Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva, GIMP, Figma).
*   **Forensic Physics:** Alpha-channel cutout boundaries, sharp clipping masks, vector typography overlays ($\ge 3$ OCR text regions), clone-stamp repetition patches, frequency separation layers.

### 7. Procedural & Non-AI Computer Graphics (`softwareImage`, `virtualRecording`)
*   **Ontology:** Mathematical, raytraced, or rasterized rendering without deep learning models.
*   **Sub-Types:** 3D game engine captures (Unreal Engine 5 Nanite/Lumen, Unity), CAD wireframe models, architectural 3D studio visualizations (Blender Cycles, V-Ray), procedural mathematical fractals (Mandelbrot, Julia sets).

### 8. AI-Enhanced / Composite (Mix) (`compositeWithTrainedAlgorithmicMedia`)
*   **Ontology:** Authentic physical capture modified or augmented using neural network models.
*   **Sub-Types:**
    *   *Generative Inpainting / Generative Fill:* Masked region regenerated via latent diffusion (Photoshop Firefly, SD Inpainting). Creates a spatial noise discrepancy: the inpainted tile has near-zero sensor noise ($\sigma_{\text{tile}} < 0.25$), while the unedited surroundings exhibit camera noise ($\sigma_{\text{surround}} > 2.0$).
    *   *Generative Outpainting / Uncropping:* Expanding canvas boundaries beyond physical capture limits.
    *   *Deepfake Face-Swapping:* InsightFace, SimSwap, LivePortrait, DeepFaceLab grafting a donor face onto a target body. Exhibits facial perimeter blending seams, inconsistent skin texture resolution, and eye-reflection divergence.
    *   *Neural Super-Resolution / Hallucinatory Upscaling:* Magnific AI, Topaz Photo AI, AuraSR hallucinating micro-textures (pores, eyelashes, fabric weave) that lack physical optical continuity.
    *   *Neural Relighting:* IC-Light (Illumination Control) altering ambient normal vectors and casting artificial directional shadows.

### 9. Fully AI-Generated (`trainedAlgorithmicMedia`)
*   **Ontology:** Synthesized end-to-end from text prompts, noise tensors, or latent embeddings via foundation models (Flux.1, Midjourney v6.1, SD 3.5, Google Imagen 3, DALL-E 3, Recraft v3).
*   **Forensic Physics:**
    *   *Azimuthal Spectral Decay:* Fourier radial decay alpha diverges from natural photographic laws ($\alpha < 1.4$ or $\alpha > 3.4$).
    *   *Bilateral Surface Smoothness:* Excessively smoothed pixel gradients lacking Poisson shot noise.
    *   *Synthetic Provenance Signals:* Algorithmic metadata (`trainedAlgorithmicMedia`), Google SynthID watermarks, or C2PA synthetic manifests.

### 10. Adversarially Spoofed / Anti-Forensic Media
*   **Ontology:** Generative AI media intentionally manipulated to fool forensic detectors.
*   **Techniques:** Injection of synthetic Poisson camera noise over diffusion latents, forged EXIF headers mimicking Sony/Canon/Nikon camera models, stripping C2PA manifests, adversarial perturbation patterns attacking neural classifier gradient heads.

1.  **Visible Spectrum (400–700 nm):**
    *   *Standard Bayer Filter Array (RGGB / RYYB):* Standard consumer cameras and smartphones.
    *   *True Monochrome Sensors (Leica M Monochrom, Phase One Achromatic):* Sensors manufactured without a color filter array; 100% luminance capture with zero demosaicing interpolation blur.
2.  **Infrared Imaging (700 nm – 14 µm):**
    *   *Near-Infrared (NIR 700–1000 nm):* "Wood Effect" rendering foliage bright white, atmospheric haze penetration, pitch-black skies.
    *   *Short-Wave Infrared (SWIR 1–3 µm):* Moisture/water absorption bands, silicon wafer inspection, uncovering historical art underdrawings.
    *   *Long-Wave Thermal Infrared (LWIR 8–14 µm / FLIR):* Direct detection of emitted blackbody thermal radiation; displayed in thermographic palettes (Ironbow, White-Hot, Rainbow).
3.  **Ultraviolet (UV) Imaging (200–400 nm):**
    *   *Reflected UV-A Photography:* Revealing subsurface human epidermal sun damage, flower bee-guide pollination patterns.
    *   *UV-Induced Visible Fluorescence:* Gemology, biological fluid tracing, forensic document forgery inspection.
4.  **Biomedical, Clinical, & Nanoscale Modalities:**
    *   *Medical Radiography (X-Ray):* 2D projection density absorption of bone and dense tissue.
    *   *Computed Tomography (CT):* Volumetric cross-sectional reconstruction slices calibrated in Hounsfield Units (HU).
    *   *Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI):* Radiofrequency pulse sequences mapping hydrogen proton relaxation (T1-weighted anatomical, T2-weighted fluid, FLAIR, fMRI blood oxygenation).
    *   *Ultrasound Sonography:* High-frequency acoustic pulse reflections producing B-mode acoustic speckle noise.
    *   *Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM):* Focused electron beam scans in vacuum producing high-depth-of-field nanoscale surface topography micrographs.
    *   *Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM):* Electron transmission through ultra-thin specimens imaging atomic and macromolecular lattices.
5.  **Aerospace, Remote Sensing, & Geospatial:**
    *   *Multispectral Satellite Imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat):* Discrete non-contiguous bands (Red-Edge, Coastal Aerosol, Water Vapor).
    *   *Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR):* Microwave active illumination imaging surface roughness through clouds and night, characterized by coherent radar speckle.
    *   *LiDAR Elevation Models (DEM/DSM):* Laser pulse time-of-flight point cloud rasterization, displayed as hillshade elevation models.

## Appendix C: Mathematical Physics Formulations

### 1. Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU) Residual Formulation
Given a digital grayscale image $I$, an estimate of the sensor noise residual $W$ is obtained by subtracting a denoised approximation:
$$W = I - F_{\text{Wiener}}(I)$$
For genuine optical sensors, the variance of $W$ over homogeneous non-clipped regions satisfies:
$$\sigma^2_{\text{PRNU}} = \frac{1}{|M|} \sum_{(x,y) \in M} (W(x,y) - \mu_W)^2 \ge 1.45$$
where $M = \{ (x,y) \mid |\nabla I(x,y)| < \tau_{\text{grad}} \land 8 \le I(x,y) \le 247 \}$.

### 2. Natural Image Fourier Radial Power Spectrum Decay
For physical photography, radially averaged 2D power spectral density follows a power-law distribution:
$$P(f) \propto f^{-\alpha}, \quad \text{where } \alpha \in [1.8, 2.2]$$
Generative latent diffusion models exhibit anomalous spectral decay slopes:
$$\alpha_{\text{synthetic}} < 1.4 \quad \lor \quad \alpha_{\text{synthetic}} > 3.4$$

### 3. Log-Likelihood Ratio (LLR) Bayesian Evidence Pooling
Multiple forensic observations $x_i$ (deep network score, PRNU variance, ELA metric, metadata presence) are fused into a calibrated posterior log-odds ratio:
$$\mathcal{L} = \ln \left( \frac{P(\text{AI})}{P(\text{Real})} \right) = \sum_{i=1}^N w_i \ln \left( \frac{P(x_i \mid \text{AI})}{P(x_i \mid \text{Real})} \right)$$
The fused posterior probability is obtained via the logistic sigmoid transform:
$$P(\text{AI} \mid \mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\mathcal{L}}}$$
